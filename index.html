How to use this tool

Setting up

  1. Choose the number of players, rounds and courts (1 to 6) using the + and − buttons, or type a number directly.
  2. Type your players' names into the Player names box, one name per line. The schedule rebuilds as you type.
  3. Not happy with the pairings? Press Reshuffle for a fresh arrangement.

Reading the schedule

  • Each round shows who plays on each court. Anyone not listed on a court is under Resting for that round.
  • The schedule aims to give everyone a new partner and new opponents each round, and to share rest time evenly. The boxes at the top show how well it managed. Zero repeat partnerships is ideal.
  • If you have more courts than the players can fill (you need 4 players per court), only the courts that can be filled are used.

What the boxes mean

  • Repeat partnerships – how many times two players ended up on the same team more than once. Each extra time counts as 1. Zero means everyone had a different partner every time.
  • Repeat opponent pairs – how many times two individual players faced each other across the net after the first time, whoever their partners were. If two players are on opposite sides in three different rounds, that adds 2. With many rounds and few players this can't be zero. For example, 12 players over 12 rounds on 2 courts has a minimum of 30, so a figure close to that is as good as it gets.
  • Rest-count spread – the gap between the player who has rested most and the player who has rested least. Zero means everyone rests the same number of rounds. 1 is the best possible when rest can't be split exactly evenly.
  • Courts used per round – how many courts are in use each round. It is lower than the number you chose if there aren't enough players to fill every court (4 players per court).
  • Repeat pair matchups – lists any two-person team that played the same two-person opposing team more than once, with the number of times (for example ×2). If nothing is listed, no team has met the same opposing team twice.
  • Times each player faced the same opposing pair again – for each player, how many times they played against the same two opponents as a pair more than once, even if their own partner was different. If nothing is listed, nobody has faced the same opposing pair twice.

Scoring

  • Enter the points each team won in the two small boxes under each match. Every player on a team gets those points added to their own total.
  • The Standings table at the bottom updates as you go. The top 6 are highlighted and labelled 1st to 6th. Players on equal points share a position.
  • Played counts only matches that have a score entered.

Saving and resetting

  • Save results stores your schedule, names and scores on this device, so they are still here if you close the page and come back. Nothing is sent anywhere, so other people cannot see your tournament.
  • Reset clears all scores but keeps the same pairings.
  • Changing the number of players, rounds, courts or names, or pressing Reshuffle, creates a new schedule and clears any scores. Set these up before the tournament starts.

Sharing this tool

  • Press Save a copy to share to download a blank copy as a single file. Send it to anyone and they can open it in any web browser, on a phone or computer, with no install or sign-up. Each person's tournament stays separate.
