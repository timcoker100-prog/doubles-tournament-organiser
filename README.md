# Doubles Tournament Organiser

A free, single-file tool for running a doubles round-robin at a tennis or padel club. It builds a fair rota across 1 to 6 courts, gives everyone new partners and opponents wherever possible, shares rest time evenly, and keeps a live points table.

**Live version:** https://timcoker100-prog.github.io/doubles-tournament-organiser/

## What it does

- Works for any number of players, rounds and courts (1 to 6)
- Tries to avoid repeat partners and repeat opponents, and shows how well it managed
- Spreads rest evenly when there are more players than court places
- Enter each match's points; every player on a team gets that team's points added to their total
- Live standings, with the top 6 highlighted and labelled 1st to 6th (tied players share a position)
- Save results on your own device, or Reset to clear the scores
- Built-in "How to use this tool" section explaining every box on the screen

## Use it

Open the live link above in any browser, on a phone or a computer. There is nothing to install and no sign-up.

## Get your own copy

Either:

- Click **Save a copy to share (.html)** at the top of the tool. This downloads a blank copy as a single file you can send to anyone or put on your own club's website; or
- Download `index.html` from this repository.

Your copy is completely independent. Nothing you do with it affects this version or anyone else's.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Player names, schedules and scores are stored only on your own device (browser local storage) and are never sent anywhere. Nobody else can see your tournament.

## Put it on your own website

Most website builders (Google Sites, Wix, Squarespace, WordPress and others) have an "Embed code" or "Custom HTML" block. Paste the full contents of `index.html` into it and publish. If the tool looks cut off, make the embed box taller, as embed blocks usually have a fixed height.
